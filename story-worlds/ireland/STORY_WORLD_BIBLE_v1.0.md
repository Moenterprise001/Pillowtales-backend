## 2. World identity statement

Ireland is a land where stories are treasured, music travels on the wind, ancient places hold quiet magic, kindness and courage matter, and every hill, lake, forest and shore may hide another adventure.

## 3. Core promise

The child visits a living Irish storytelling world.

The child may:

- lead an original adventure
- travel beside a legendary figure
- witness a defining canon moment

The child must never replace the legendary figure or rewrite the defining event.

No story may require prior knowledge of the source legend.

## 10. Cultural boundaries

Never:

- reduce Ireland to leprechauns, shamrocks and pots of gold
- mock Irish names, accents or language
- turn political or historical conflict into bedtime content
- present invented additions as authentic canon
- replace legendary heroes with the child
- make sacred or important figures slapstick caricatures
- use graphic violence or horror
- require the child to answer direct questions
- imply that one version of a legend is the only authentic version

## 11. Pronunciation requirement

No Irish Story World may publish until major names and places have been:

- recorded with authentic display spelling
- supplied with IPA where practical
- supplied with provider-tested narration hints
- reviewed by an appropriate Irish-language or local pronunciation reviewer
- tested through every active narration provider

The pronunciation entries themselves are not yet approved in this draft.
