# Story World Bible Template v1.0

## 1. Identity

- Name
- Slug
- Region
- Countries
- Peoples or cultures
- Traditions
- Category
- World type
- Age range
- Version
- Publication status

## 2. World identity statement

One paragraph describing what makes the world recognisable and why children should want to return.

## 3. Purpose and cultural scope

Define what is included, what is excluded, and the sources or traditions that shape the world.

## 4. Atmosphere

- intended feeling
- landscapes
- seasons
- colours
- sounds
- weather
- daily rhythm

## 5. Geography library

For every recurring location record:

- name
- category: real, canon, living-world original
- atmosphere
- suitable ages
- nearby places
- recurring characters
- creatures
- story opportunities
- restrictions

## 6. Character library

For every recurring character record:

- identity
- role
- personality
- what they love
- what they are good at
- what they hope for
- how they help others
- canon restrictions
- allowed narrative types

## 7. Canon library

For every canon story record:

- title
- concise summary
- defining events that must not change
- characters
- places
- core values
- age range
- bedtime adaptation
- companion-story possibilities
- living-world expansion possibilities
- continuation or prequel boundaries

## 8. Story DNA

- openings
- triggers
- challenges
- discoveries
- resolutions
- bedtime endings
- humour
- recurring motifs
- signature moments
- restricted patterns
- anti-repetition rules

## 9. Narrative mix

- living world
- companion
- canon/legendary moment

Percentages must total 100.

## 10. Editorial rules

- reading-age rules
- tone
- dialogue
- pacing
- magic
- conflict
- endings
- prior-knowledge accessibility
- cultural boundaries
- things never to do

## 11. Pronunciation pack

For every name, place, creature, festival or important term:

- display spelling
- language
- IPA
- readable phonetic hint
- provider-specific override
- usage notes
- verifier
- verification date

Authentic display spelling must remain in the story. Pronunciation substitutions are narration-only.

## 12. Translation notes

- words never translated
- names preserved
- culturally sensitive terms
- fallback wording
- language-specific pronunciation notes

## 13. Assets

- cover
- thumbnail
- icon
- colours
- illustration style
- narrator preferences
- future ambience or music

## 14. QA release gate

- cultural review passed
- canon review passed
- bedtime review passed
- native review passed
- translation review passed
- pronunciation review passed
- narration QA passed
- story-quality QA passed
- performance regression test passed
