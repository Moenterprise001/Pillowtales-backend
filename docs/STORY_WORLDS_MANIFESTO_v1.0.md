# PillowTales Story Worlds Manifesto v1.0

## Purpose

Story Worlds exist to turn bedtime into a gentle journey through the world's storytelling traditions.

## Core promise

**Respect the canon. Expand the world.**

PillowTales preserves the identity of traditional stories while creating original, personalised bedtime adventures around them.

## Principles

1. Learn before creating.
2. Respect cultures and avoid stereotypes.
3. Preserve defining canon events.
4. Let children belong in the world without replacing its legendary heroes.
5. Keep every story age-appropriate, safe, warm and bedtime-ready.
6. Make prior knowledge unnecessary.
7. Treat pronunciation as a release requirement.
8. Use AI as the storyteller, not as the cultural source of truth.
9. Keep Story Worlds data-driven and free of world-specific engine code.
10. Leave children wanting to visit the world again.

## Quality ownership

PillowTales owns:

- research
- Story World Bibles
- canon libraries
- Story DNA
- editorial rules
- pronunciation packs
- cultural review
- bedtime review
- narration QA

AI owns:

- personalisation
- variation
- age adaptation
- language generation
- final storytelling within the approved framework
